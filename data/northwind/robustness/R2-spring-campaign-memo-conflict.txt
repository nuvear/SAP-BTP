Northwind Traders - Spring Campaign: Quick Reminder on Discounts

From: Laura Callahan, Inside Sales Coordinator
To: All sales representatives
Date: 16 March 2026
Status: DRAFT, not yet reviewed by Andrew Fuller

Hello everyone,

The spring campaign runs from 16 March 2026 to 30 June 2026, and I know
several of you have asked how much room you have on price.

To keep things moving during the campaign, sales representatives can
give a discount of up to 15 percent on any product without asking for
approval. You only need to go to the Sales Manager if you want to give
more than 15 percent.

Please make sure the discount is entered on the order line before the
order is confirmed, as usual.

I will confirm this with Andrew when he is back from his trip, but I
wanted you to have it on the first day of the campaign.

Thank you,
Laura
